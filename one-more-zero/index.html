ONE MORE ZERO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

GraftForge Games ("we," "our," or "us") operates the One More Zero mobile application (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

Where required, advertising consent is requested separately. Playing the game does not itself mean you consent to personalised advertising.

1. Direct Data Collection

The core game does not require an account, name, email address or precise location. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a developer-controlled server storing your individual game saves.

If you voluntarily contact support or share a diagnostic export, we use the information you send to respond to your request. Our lawful basis for handling voluntary support correspondence is our legitimate interest in providing support and resolving problems. We may also retain records where needed to meet a legal obligation.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

One More Zero includes optional rewarded advertisements. The advertising services differ by installed version:

  • Versions up to and including 00.01.03: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) serves rewarded advertisements.
  • From version 00.01.04: Unity LevelPlay mediates rewarded advertisements, initially through Unity Ads. AdMob is not configured as a rewarded-ad serving network in this version.
  • Consent management from 00.01.04: Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) continues to display and record applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. Google Mobile Ads SDK components remain bundled for this purpose, although AdMob is no longer the rewarded-ad provider.

Advertising SDKs and consent services may process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID and App Set ID), IP-derived approximate location, advertising impressions and interactions, technical performance and error information, and fraud-prevention data. The details depend on the provider, location, settings and applicable consent choices. Where consent is legally required for advertising processing, the relevant consent choices are requested separately; providers explain any other applicable lawful bases in their own policies.

Relevant privacy policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps using its services

3. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your card or bank account details. The app receives purchase and entitlement information needed to apply and restore purchased features, and to reconcile consumable purchases.

4. Network Communications and Security

The app contacts an external trusted-time service (timeapi.io) over HTTPS to validate offline progress. The time service can receive your IP address and standard network-request information, but the request does not include your game save or account information. Advertising and billing SDKs communicate with their own providers under the security practices described in their respective policies.

Advertising, billing, consent and trusted-time providers may process information outside the UK. Where international transfers occur, the relevant provider's privacy information explains its handling of those transfers and any safeguards. Contact us if you need help locating that information.

5. Children's Privacy

One More Zero is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Age-related privacy obligations and Google Play target-audience requirements are reviewed as part of each release.

6. Managing Advertising and Privacy Choices

Where applicable, the game presents an advertising consent message and provides Settings → Privacy Choices when the consent platform indicates a privacy-options entry point is required. You may review or withdraw choices using that form. Availability depends on the published consent message and the provider's service.

In version 00.01.04, Google UMP manages the form while Unity LevelPlay handles rewarded advertisements. The app checks the consent provider's ad-request eligibility before initializing LevelPlay. A recorded consent response does not necessarily mean you opted into personalised advertising. Declining consent does not prevent normal gameplay but may affect ad availability.

You can also manage your device's advertising identifier and related settings in Android. Those settings are not always a substitute for the in-app consent choices. If the form is unavailable, contact us using the details below.

7. Analytics, Diagnostics & Crash Reporting

One More Zero does not currently use a separate developer-controlled analytics or crash-reporting service. Integrated advertising, billing and platform services may process their own diagnostic, performance, usage and anti-fraud information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game progress remains on your device until cleared through application functionality, app storage settings or uninstall, subject to any device backup behaviour. Support emails and diagnostic exports sent to us are held for as long as reasonably needed to respond and resolve the issue, or longer where necessary for legal obligations or disputes; we do not set a universal fixed retention period for all enquiries. Third-party providers apply their own retention and deletion practices. Contact us if you need assistance identifying the relevant provider.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where applicable. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights. The app has no developer-run user-account database. We can help direct requests concerning third-party advertising or billing information to the relevant provider.

If you have concerns about how your personal data is handled, you can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
