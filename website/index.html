WEBSITE PRIVACY

Website privacy

Draft for owner review — not approved for production. This notice describes the proposed storefront release. Hosting configuration, analytics settings and policy wording must be confirmed before publication.

Scope

This notice covers the GraftForge Games website and privacy website. The One More Zero application privacy policy separately describes processing in the Android game.

Website requests

Hosting infrastructure processes connection information, such as IP addresses and HTTP requests, to deliver the websites, maintain security and diagnose failures. Cloudflare is the previously recorded hosting provider; the current deployment configuration and any hosting-level analytics require owner confirmation.

Fonts and gameplay media

The proposed website release serves fonts, images, the Google Play QR code and silent gameplay preview from its own domain. The privacy website also serves its fonts locally.

Analytics

The reviewed storefront source contains no developer-controlled visitor analytics or advertising tracker integration. This does not establish whether analytics or other processing is enabled through the hosting configuration.

Optional YouTube playback

The proposed storefront does not embed a YouTube player until a visitor chooses to play a trailer. Loading the player connects the browser to YouTube and associated Google services, which may process IP addresses, device and browser information and playback activity. Privacy-enhanced embedding does not eliminate all third-party processing. Read the Google privacy policy.

External links and contact

Google Play, Discord and other external links lead to independently operated services with their own privacy policies. If you contact the studio, your message and contact details are used to respond to your enquiry. For questions about website data or privacy, contact studio@graftforge.games.
